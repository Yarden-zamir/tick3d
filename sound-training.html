    How the ear training works

    Each cell of the board has its own sound. The sound has three parts: one for the layer, one for the row and one for the column. Hear a cell and find its place.

    • A new sound comes as a learn card first. The card shows the answer, and the board lights the whole layer, row or column.
    • After two learn cards, the sound joins the quiz. A quiz card hides the answer. Tap any cell in the area that you hear, then Check.
    • A right answer moves the sound to a later box, so it comes back less often. A wrong answer brings it back soon.
    • The part that you hear least well gets more cards. When every sound is in the quiz, full cells come too: find the exact cell. They come more often as you get better.
    • The trainer uses the sound set of the game, or Cells when the game uses Classic. Change it with the note button. Headphones help with the columns.
    • Your progress stays in this browser.

    Sound set

    The game uses the same set. The play button of a set plays four moves of it. Classic does not name cells, so the trainer uses Cells.

    Reset your ear training? Every sound starts as new again.
