    About these stats

    Totals and player names only, never a game or a code.

    • Everyone: the games of all players. Mine: your games on every device that you logged in with, or on this browser.
    • The filters go into the address. Share the address to share the view.
    • A game counts when it ends. A Nearby game counts once.
    • Win rate: wins in your last 20 games. Friend games do not count.
    • Times and days are in UTC. Everyone numbers refresh once a minute.
    • Hover a bar or a cell for its exact number.
