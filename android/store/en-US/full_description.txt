tick3d is tic-tac-toe in three dimensions. The board is a tower of four 4×4 layers. Get four marks in a straight line to win: along a row or a column, up through the layers, or diagonally through the whole cube.

Ways to play
• Against the computer, from easy to hard.
• With a friend on one screen.
• Online: your app purchase lets you host. Share a code or a link; friends join free in a browser and chat during the game.
• Nearby: devices on the same Wi-Fi find each other. Hosting and joining Nearby games stay free.

More to explore
• Every cell has its own sound. Play a finished game back as a song.
• Play by voice: hold a note to pick a cell. Calibrate and practise in the Voice room. The microphone audio stays on your device.
• Time limits that work like a chess clock.
• Stats for every game, and a history of your own games.
• Twelve themes, from light and dark to retro and synthwave.

No ads. No trackers. No in-app purchases.
