    How the tesseract works

    This is an experiment. The board has a fourth coordinate: four cubes of 4×4×4, side by side. A cell is cube, layer, row, column.

    • Four in a straight line wins. A line can stay in one cube, or step through all four cubes: the same cell in every cube, or a diagonal that moves one step in the cube each time. There are 520 lines.
    • Pick a cube in the map, then play in it. The map shows every mark of all four cubes.
    • Point at a cell to see every cell that shares a line with it, in all cubes.
    • The 4D view draws all four cubes at once as a tesseract: cube 1 innermost, cube 4 outermost. Every winning line is a straight line in it. Spin it in 4D to see the cubes turn through each other, drag to turn it in 3D, and tap a cell to play.
    • An open threat is an empty cell that completes three marks of one player. The map rings them in that player's colour.
    • The game stays in this browser. It is not saved to My games, and online play does not offer it yet.
