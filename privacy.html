Last change: 8 October 2026

tick3d is a game by Yarden Zamir. This policy covers the website and the Android app. The app shows the same website.

tick3d has no ads, no trackers and no analytics services. It does not sell your data or give it to others.

What the server stores

  • Player token. A random id that the game makes on your first visit. It holds no name, email or device id. Your browser keeps it and sends it with online calls.
  • Custom name. The name that you choose, if you choose one.
  • Online sessions. The moves, the seats, the session name and the chat. A session keeps its last 50 chat messages.
  • Game results and stats. Every finished game, also games against the computer, with a friend and Nearby. A game also stores counts about how it was played: the device kind (phone, tablet or computer), the theme, the view, and how many moves came from the board or the keypad.
  • Voice room runs. The mode, the times and the score of a finished practice run. No audio.
  • GitHub login, only if you log in: your GitHub id, login name and picture address. A login cookie keeps you logged in for 90 days.
  • Error reports. The error message and the app version, without your token.

Nearby uses your network address to find devices on the same Wi-Fi. The server keeps it in memory only while your Nearby game is open, and never writes it to disk.

What stays on your device

  • Microphone audio. Voice play reads the microphone on your device only. The audio and the voice clips for a replay stay in memory on the device. The game never uploads or saves them.
  • Your settings, your voice calibration, and the games of this device, in the storage of your browser.

Who sees what

  • Anyone with the code or link of an online session sees its players, moves and chat.
  • Anyone with the link to a finished game sees its moves and the player names.
  • The Stats page shows totals of all games, without names.
  • Your GitHub picture loads from GitHub.

All data goes over HTTPS.

How long it stays

  • An online session without a move: deleted after 9 hours.
  • Everything else: until you ask to delete it.

Delete your data

  • Clear history in My games hides your games from your history. The games still count in the totals.
  • Reset to generated name in My games removes your custom name.
  • Log out unlinks this browser from your GitHub account.
  • To delete the data on your device, clear the site data of tick3d in your browser, or uninstall the app.
  • To delete all your data on the server, email dev@yarden-zamir.com. Give your player name or GitHub login, and a game link if you have one. The data is deleted within 30 days.

Children

tick3d is for players of 13 and older.

Contact

Questions and requests: dev@yarden-zamir.com.
