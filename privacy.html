Privacy

My games GitHub

In short

  • No ads, no trackers.
  • Your mic audio never leaves your device.
  • We store your games and chat to make the game work.
  • Delete everything any time: My games → Delete my data.
  • Questions: dev@yarden-zamir.com

What the server stores

  • Player token: a random id that your browser makes. No name, email or device id.
  • Games: moves, results, chat (the last 50 messages of a session), and how a game was played (device kind, theme, view, board or keypad).
  • Custom name, if you set one.
  • GitHub login, if you log in: id, login and picture address. The login cookie lasts 90 days.
  • Voice room runs: mode, times and score. No audio.
  • Reports and blocks that you make, with a copy of the reported message. A log keeps each moderator action.
  • Error reports: the error, the file and the app version, without your token.

Nearby uses your network address to find devices on your Wi-Fi. It stays in memory and is never saved.

Who sees what

  • Anyone with a session code sees its players, moves and chat. A game link shows the moves and names.
  • Other players see your person id, a short hash of your token, never the token.
  • The maintainers read reports, and can hide a message or clear a name.
  • The Stats page shows totals, and a leaderboard with the name and game results of each player. Hide my stats in My games takes you off the leaderboard.

How long it stays

  • A session without a move: 9 hours.
  • Reports, the moderation log and error reports: 90 days.
  • Deletion notices (the person id of a player who deleted their data): 1 year.
  • Server logs: no log of requests, at most 30 MB per service. The oldest lines go first.
  • Everything else: until you delete it.

Delete your data

  • Delete my data in My games deletes your data at once (on every device of a GitHub account). It logs you out. Games with other players stay for them, without you. Your chat messages read "Deleted by its author."
  • A deletion notice removes your name and chat lines from other players' devices when they next go online.
  • Your device keeps a random id with no data attached, so that blocks by other players keep working. Your settings stay unless you reset them.
  • The button cannot reach: devices not logged in to the same account (press the button on each), devices that stay offline for a year, links and pictures already shared, and reports about you (kept 90 days for safety).
  • In My games: Clear history hides your games, Reset to generated name removes your name, Unblock removes a block.
  • On your device: clear the site data of tick3d, or uninstall the app.
  • If the button cannot reach your data, email dev@yarden-zamir.com with your GitHub login or person id. The data is deleted within 30 days.

tick3d is a game by Yarden Zamir, for players of 13 and older. This policy covers the website and the Android app. All data goes over HTTPS. Last change: 8 October 2026.
