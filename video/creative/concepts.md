# Concepts

All three keep the hard limits: 8 bars at 136 BPM plus the ringing chord, at most 3 lines, the game's own sounds, a legal seeded game.

## A. "The cube is the instrument"

- Bars 1–2: one cell at a time lights and sounds, layer by layer, like a keyboard.
- Bars 3–6: a fast game. Each move plays its note, and the notes form the song of the game.
- Bars 7–8: the song plays back. End card.
- Lines: "Every cell has a sound." / "Every game is a song." / the URL.
- Weak point: it sells the sound first. A viewer with the sound off sees a slow light show and no game.

## B. "76 lines"

- Bar 1: the empty cube. All 76 lines flash in turn, faster and faster.
- Bars 2–6: a game, with each threat drawn as a line.
- Bars 7–8: the win and the end card.
- Lines: "4 in a row." / "76 ways to win." / the URL.
- Weak point: clear, but it is a diagram. The camera stays outside, so it looks like a tutorial.

## C. "Thread the cube" (chosen)

The camera does what no screen capture can: it goes inside the tower.

- Bar 1: the four layers slam in on the four beats, one Classic note each.
- Bar 2: the camera dives down the centre of the tower, through all four layers. "4 in a row. In 3D."
- Bar 3: the camera breaks out and orbits. The game doubles its speed, and each piece pulses with its own note. The theme sweeps across the frame.
- Bar 4: X takes corner 0. All 76 lines flash for two beats: "76 ways to win." Two cells blink: a double threat. O can block one.
- Bar 5: X takes the other. The win runs from corner to corner of the cube, cells 0–21–42–63, as a beam of light. The camera rides the beam out of the top corner.
- Bar 6: confetti. The whole game plays back as a song, each piece pulsing on its note.
- Bars 7–8: the tower shrinks into the end card, and the URL slams in. The layers pulse up into the final chord.

## Why C

- **It is the boldest, and it still sells one clear idea.** Every bold move shows depth: the dive shows four layers, the beam crosses all four. The impossible camera is the proposition, not decoration.
- **It makes people want to play.** Bar 4 teaches the one trick that makes the game fun: two threats at once, and the opponent can block only one. A viewer sees the trick and wants to try it. The beam is the reward that the viewer wants to make happen.
- **It keeps A and B as support.** B's "76 ways to win" is line 2, and A's song is the soundtrack and the bar 6 replay. Sound off, the story still reads. Sound on, every hit is a real cell note.
- **The game is real.** Seed 214 of medium against medium self-play wins on the main space diagonal `[0, 21, 42, 63]`. That diagonal is also the demo line of every sound set in `src/sound.ts` (`DEMO_CELLS`).
