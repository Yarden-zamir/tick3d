Test tick3d before everyone else

Thanks for helping! It takes about two minutes. Which phone do you have?

Android

  1. Join the Google Group

    Use the same Google account as on your phone.

    Open the group

  2. Become a tester

    Tap "Become a tester", then come back to this page. Right after you join the group, Play can take a few minutes before this works.

    Do not download yet. Play shows a price until you redeem your free code in the next step.

    Become a tester

  3. Claim a free code, then redeem it

    The app costs money on Play. Pick one card for a free code. Redeem it in Play, or in the Play Store app: profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Redeem code.

    Loading the codes…

    No codes left right now. Message Yarden at dev@yarden-zamir.com with your Google Play email, and you get one by hand.

  4. Install

    After you redeem the code, open tick3d on Play. It shows "Install" with no price. If Play still shows a price, redeem your code first.

    Install from Play

iPhone

  1. Install TestFlight

    TestFlight is the free Apple app for test builds.

    TestFlight on the App Store

  2. Open the invite on your iPhone

    Tap Accept, then Install. On a computer? Scan the code with the iPhone camera.

    Open the invite

  3. Keep TestFlight

    New builds come through TestFlight. A test build stops after 90 days, so keep TestFlight to get the next one.

    How testing works

    tick3d is in a closed test on Google Play and on TestFlight.

    • Android: each tester gets one free Google Play code. A card shows only the first letters of its code.
    • A claim stores the email and the name that you type. Other testers see the name and your Gravatar picture, never the email.
    • This browser keeps your code, so it shows here on your next visit.
    • iPhone: the TestFlight invite needs no code.
