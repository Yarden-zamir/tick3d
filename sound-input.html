    Previews

      How the Voice room works

      Turn on the microphone, then whistle, hum or sing a steady note. Your pitch lights a cell of the board. The sound stays in this browser.

      • The rail is your range, low at the left and high at the right. It holds the 64 cells: four rows of 16. A higher pitch lights a higher row. Inside a row, a slide up moves from layer 1, column 1 to layer 4, column 4.
      • The default range is 150 to 2400 Hz, one octave for each row: hum for rows 4 and 3, whistle for rows 2 and 1. Calibrate to your voice: make your lowest comfortable sound for 2 seconds, then your highest. The rows then split your own range. Cancel or the back button leaves the calibration with no change. Reset to default goes back.
      • Range and spread, under Settings: type the low and high ends, or hold a note and tap Use my note. The spread says how the 64 cells share the range: the same musical interval for each cell, the same Hz, more room in the middle, or the game notes (the layer is the nearest of C, D, E and G, and each row is one octave). A second after a change, each cell shows its frequency in Hz for a moment.
      • Stickiness keeps a held cell lit when your pitch wobbles. It grows while you hold the cell. The band on the rail shows it.
      • Tilt, on a phone or a tablet: your voice picks the place, and a small front-to-back tilt moves it up to 4 cells up or down. Tip the top edge toward you to go up. The arrow on the rail shows the nudge. Settings has the strength, Recentre and an off switch. A computer has no tilt.
      • Free play: hold a note on one cell for 1 second to place an X. The X color fills the cell while you hold. Tap a cell to hear it in the sound set of the game.
      • The board has the views and layouts of the game, under Settings: the tower (drag to turn it) or flat, in a grid, side by side, top to bottom or in steps. The game and this room share the choice. When your voice moves to a layer out of view, the page scrolls to it.
      • Targets: a cell lights with a dashed ring. Slide until your light sits on it and hold it. Hot and cold tell you how near you are. 10 targets against the clock.
      • Echo: the game plays the sound of a cell, as the ear training does. Find it by ear, then sing to it and hold it. 100 points for the right cell, 25 fewer for each step off. 8 rounds.
      • Levels: Easy keeps targets near each other with a short hold. Hard spreads them over the board, holds longer, and has no stickiness. Each mode and level keeps its own best, here and on the leaderboard.
      • Playoff: open the Voice room from an online game (the Voice room link in the game panel). The other player gets an invite. You both sing to the same targets at the same time, and the faster total wins.
      • The game uses the same range and stickiness when you play by voice. Train your ear to learn the cell sounds.
