      How the Voice room works

      Turn on the mic, then whistle, hum or sing a steady note. Your pitch lights a cell. The sound stays on this device.

      • The rail is your range: low at the left, high at the right. A higher pitch lights a higher row.
      • Calibrate to your voice: hold your lowest note, then your highest. The rows then split your range.
      • Stickiness keeps a held cell lit when your pitch wobbles.
      • On a phone, a small tilt nudges the cell up or down.
      • Free play: hold a note 1 s to place an X. Tap a cell to hear it.
      • Targets: hold each lit target, against the clock.
      • Echo: hear a cell, then sing it back.
      • Playoff: open the Voice room from an online game. The other player gets an invite, and the faster total wins.
      • The game uses the same range when you play by voice. Train your ear to learn the cell sounds.
