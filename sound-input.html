How it works

The note picks the layer: C, D, E or G, the pitches of the game's layers. A slide up moves the light right along the columns and on to the next layer. A higher octave lights a higher row, so whistle for the top rows and hum for the bottom rows.

Tap a cell to hear the game's sound for it. Headphones keep that sound out of the microphone.

The sound stays on your device: the page reads the microphone in the browser and sends nothing.

Train your ear to learn the cell sounds of the game.
