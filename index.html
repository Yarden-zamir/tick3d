                    How to win
                    
      
                    Get four marks in a straight line: along a row or column, up through the layers, or diagonally through the cube.
                    
      
                      
        
                    • Drag around the tower to turn it.
                      • 
        
                    • Keypad: tap the layer, row and column, then Place.
                      • 
        
                    • Every cell has its own sound. The arrow next to the speaker opens the sound sets and their maps.
                      • 
        
                    • Time limits work like a chess clock. A player who runs out loses.
                      • 
        
                    • Lock stops setting changes until the game ends.
                      • 
        
                    • Online: share the code or link. Nearby: devices on the same Wi-Fi.
                      • 
        
                    • Players: any player manages the seats. A change that affects the other player, and an undo, wait until that player accepts.
                      • 
        
                    • Hover or long-press an icon to see what it does.
