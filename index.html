        How to play

        Take turns to place a mark. Get four in a straight line to win.

        Lines can run along a layer, up through the layers, or diagonally through the cube. There are 76 lines.

        Point at a cell to light up the cells above and below it.

        Time limits work like a chess clock. Set a limit for each player's whole game, a limit per move, or both. A player who runs out of either limit loses. The first move of each player is untimed. A change during a game starts with the next game.

        Lock settings keeps every setting, the view included, until the game ends. Online, the lock and the hide options apply to both players.

        Advanced: computer player

        Every number behind the computer. Easy and medium tire in a long game: each value moves from fresh to tired between two moves. A changed computer keeps its own records.
