    How to play

    Take turns to place a mark. Get four in a straight line to win.

    Lines can run along a layer, up through the layers, or diagonally through the cube. There are 76 lines.

    Point at a cell to light up the cells above and below it.

    Lock settings keeps every setting, the view included, until the game ends. Online, the lock and the hide options apply to both players.
